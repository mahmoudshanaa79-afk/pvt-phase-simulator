# OpenPhase v1.6.0

**Status: prepared for the owner's approval. Not released or tagged.**

v1.6 makes the saturation solver find roots it used to miss. It adds a kij
study, the owner-approved optional kij input and a fourth teaching lab, and it
explains the solver's codes in plain language. The app was also checked for
crashes, speed and ease of use.

## Engine: roots the solver used to miss (P7, P8)

Both changes were approved by the owner and applied on 1 November 2026.

- **P7: refine boundaries when the scan finds no root.** The boundary walk now
  also runs when the pressure grid finds no root at all. In the May (2015)
  record, all nine `not_found` bubble states now converge:
  - CH4+C2 bubble: 11 → 17 of 17;
  - CH4+C3 bubble: 20 → 23 of 23.

  A root that is found but cannot be bracketed is reported as
  `SATURATION_ROOT_UNBRACKETED`, with no number.
- **P8: check past a failed walk step.** Where the walk stops at a failed
  evaluation, a bounded Newton solve looks at the rest of the interval. A root
  it finds can only make a result ambiguous. It is never reported as the
  pressure.
  - CH4+C2 dew 008, 009, 013 and 017 have a second root and are now ambiguous.
  - CH4+C2 dew changes from 12 states at 5.34 % AARD to 8 states at 2.07 %.
  - The former worst result, 013 at −25.8 %, was the lower of two roots.
- The validation record, the Newton comparison and the P3 study were
  regenerated on the new engine.
- Every ambiguous dew state is confirmed independently, without the
  production verdict. The tool is `tools/run_hidden_dew_root_check.py`, and the
  route used per state is recorded.
- The extended-envelope survey now closes 14 of 16 cases.

## kij study (P6) and the optional kij input (Option 2)

- `docs/investigations/kij_study.md` fits one constant kij per binary to the
  measured bubble pressures:
  - CH4+C2: 0;
  - CH4+C3: +0.0021 (fixed-set bubble AARD 0.99 % → 0.68 % over 22 states).

  A constant kij does not reduce the ambiguous dew states. Provisional butane
  evidence is reported separately.
- The owner approved **Option 2**: kij = 0 stays the default and the validation
  reference, and the app offers an optional, clearly labelled kij input.
  - A collapsed *Advanced: binary interaction (kij)* expander has one field per
    pair (absent pairs disabled).
  - The kij reaches every engine call the app makes.
  - A non-zero kij shows a *user-supplied kij* badge and is stated in results,
    exports, reports and saved cases.
  - At kij = 0 the app passes no interactions to the engine, so the kij input
    changes no result, export or saved case.
  - Case files gain schema 1.1.0, written only when a kij is non-zero. Older
    files load as kij = 0.

## App: plain-language solver codes

- `BOUNDARY_SATURATION_ROOT`, `HIDDEN_SATURATION_ROOT` +
  `MULTIPLE_SATURATION_ROOTS` and `SATURATION_ROOT_UNBRACKETED` each get one
  short sentence, always next to the engine's code.
- They appear on the Phase Envelope and Diagnostics pages, in a Validation-page
  table, in the export and in the HTML report. The export schema is now 1.2.0.
- The Phase Envelope action also solves the bubble and dew pressure at the
  operating temperature, and lists the incipient phase.

## App: robustness, speed and ease of use

- **No crashes on hostile input.** A new robustness suite
  (`tests/test_app_robustness*.py`) drives every page with edge inputs, every
  unit, kij edges, odd sweeps, broken case files and every RUN action with each
  shipped example. Three crashes were fixed:
  - the form now enforces a supported input range of **50–1000 K and
    1 Pa–1 GPa**, stated in the user's unit; outside it the engine raised
    instead of returning a status;
  - if an engine call still raises, the page shows the engine's own reason;
  - a deeply nested case file is refused cleanly.
- **Faster repeats.** Engine results are shared from a bounded cache instead
  of being copied on every hit, so repeating an envelope takes 0.1 s instead of
  3–5 s. Sweeps are cached too. Cold, warm and direct results and exports are
  byte-identical (`tests/test_app_performance.py`). The first envelope of a
  case is engine time (about 2–3 minutes); engine proposal P9 waits for the
  owner.
- **Easier to start and to read.**
  - Overview opens with a short "Start here" box and shows the result before
    the model panel.
  - The envelope page gives an honest time estimate.
  - Empty states name the button to press.
  - The sidebar starts closed on phones.
  - The composition table shows 6 significant figures with mol %.
  - The phase split is stated in words.
  - Learn mode links the four labs.
  - On Diagnostics, "Show advanced raw public objects" no longer freezes the
    page after a phase envelope. The envelope (about 40 MB as JSON) is shown
    as a summary, with the full object as a download.

  The review is `docs/investigations/usability_review.md`.
- **Deployment health.** CI now runs the app with only the deployed
  dependencies. The known-limits panel names the validation source and the
  input range. `docs/STREAMLIT_APPLICATION.md` has reboot steps and a
  post-reboot checklist.

- `tools/profile_app.py` measures start-up, page renders, every action and the

## Known limits

- **One data source.** Every validation state and the kij fit come from May et
  al. (2015). The fitted kij is calibration, not validation.
- **Dew points.** CH4+C3 has a unique dew answer at 1 of 23 measured states. At
  20 of them PR has two dew pressures and the app reports none, by design.
- **Butane constants are provisional** (CoolProp). The butanes are not admitted
  until the owner supplies the Yang & Richter (2025) values
  (`docs/BUTANES_ADMISSION_PLAN.md`).
- Published PR kij values could not be checked against the literature from the
  web session.
- The model has no volume translation, so liquid densities are not
  quantitative.
- **The first phase envelope of a case takes minutes** (engine time). Proposal
  P9 in `docs/ENGINE_CHANGE_PROPOSALS.md` would roughly halve it without
  changing any result; it needs the owner's approval.
