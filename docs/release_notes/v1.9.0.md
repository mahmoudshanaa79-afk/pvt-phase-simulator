# OpenPhase v1.9.0

v1.9 makes the app say when a "stable" verdict rests only on trivial
stability trials, the near-critical limitation that v1.8 documented. This is
the owner-approved part (b) of proposal P12. The verdicts themselves, and
every other result of the Peng–Robinson engine, are unchanged.

## Engine (owner-approved, 15 November 2026)

- **A stable verdict that rests only on trivial trials is labelled.** When
  both stability trials return the feed itself and the verdict is *stable*,
  the stability result now carries the diagnostic
  `STABILITY_TRIVIAL_TRIALS_ONLY` in a new field, `verdict_diagnostics`.
- **Nothing else changes.** Every pre-existing field of every result is
  byte-identical. This was shown with the engine fingerprint over 801 cases
  (the new field left out of the comparison), the golden-master comparison
  and the regenerated validation record.
- **Part (a) of P12, extra trial starts near the feed, is not approved.** So
  50/50 methane/propane at 8.534 MPa is still reported as one stable phase
  from 314 K to 320 K, although those states lie inside the envelope. The
  result now carries the note.

## App and teaching

- **A short note under "single phase".** When the diagnostic is present, the
  Overview says: stable here means no instability was found. That is normal
  for a clear single phase; near a critical point, check the state against
  the phase envelope.
- **The Diagnostics page** lists the code with its plain-language meaning. The
  glossary now explains all 43 codes the engine can report.
- **Labs 6 and 7** explain what the note means and where it appears. Every
  number is still recomputed from the engine by a test.

## Investigations (no engine or app setting changed)

- **P10 survey.** The app's phase envelope was run on 16 cases with and
  without the corrector's global fallback:
  - accepted points and termination reasons are identical on all 32
    branches;
  - the fallback never rescued a step;
  - the envelope takes 20.7 % less time without it.

  The note is `docs/investigations/p10_survey.md`. The setting change waits
  for the owner's decision.
- **Second experimental source.** `docs/SECOND_SOURCE_PLAN.md` records the
  desktop's attempt:
  - the ThermoML search service was unavailable;
  - four candidate papers have no ThermoML record;
  - the comparison tool reproduces the validation record on the real May
    (2015) file.

  No second source has been downloaded yet.
- **Mutation testing of `adapters.py`** now covers all 884 mutants (v1.8
  stopped at a time box). New tests pin the envelope-location and error
  helpers. The score rose from 75.9 % to 95.0 %, excluding annotation-only
  mutants; the remaining survivors are listed in
  `docs/investigations/health_check_2026_11.md`.

## Known limits

- **Near-critical stability (P12).** Close to a mixture critical point, a
  "single phase" result can still be wrong. The note now says so whenever both
  trials were trivial. Check such a result against the phase envelope.
- **One experimental source.** Every validation state comes from May et al.
  (2015).
- **Dew points.** Methane + propane has a unique dew answer at 1 of 23
  measured states. At most of the others PR has two dew pressures, and the app
  reports none, by design.
- **Volume translation is not validated for mixtures.** The shifts are fitted
  to pure saturated liquids only.
- **Components C1–C3 only.** The butanes wait for the owner's Table 5 values
  (draft PR 47); CO2 and N2 exist only as a plan.
- **The first phase envelope of a case takes about one to three minutes**,
  depending on how busy the server is; repeating the same case is answered
  from the cache.
